# Twitter Analytics Dashboard - Power BI | Nullclass Internship

## 🌟 Project Overview
A comprehensive **Real-Time Twitter Analytics Dashboard** developed as part of the **Data Analyst Internship** at **Nullclass**. This project demonstrates advanced Power BI capabilities by implementing six complex analytical tasks that transform raw Twitter engagement data into actionable business intelligence.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Capabilities

### 🎯 Advanced Analytical Tasks Implemented

#### **Task 1: Media Engagement Correlation Analysis**
- Scatter plot visualizing Media Engagements vs Media Views
- Highlights tweets with >5% engagement rate
- Time-based visibility: 6 PM - 11 PM IST
- Advanced filtering: Replies > 10, Odd dates, Word count > 50

#### **Task 2: Click Performance by Content Category**
- Clustered bar chart comparing URL, Profile, and Hashtag clicks
- Categorization by Media, Links, and Hashtags
- Time-based visibility: 3 PM - 5 PM IST
- Smart filtering for optimal content strategy insights

#### **Task 3: Top Performing Tweets Identification**
- Ranking of top 10 tweets by Total Engagement (Retweets + Likes)
- Weekday-only analysis with weekend exclusion
- Comprehensive performance metrics display
- Time-based visibility: 3 PM - 5 PM IST

#### **Task 4: Engagement Rate Trend Analysis**
- Monthly trend analysis comparing Media vs Non-Media tweets
- Proper chronological sorting with dual time-window visibility
- Advanced character-based filtering and content optimization
- Time-based visibility: 3 PM - 5 PM IST & 7 AM - 11 AM IST

#### **Task 5: High Media Engagement Benchmarking**
- Comparative analysis of Replies, Retweets, and Likes
- Median-based performance thresholding
- Summer 2020 campaign focus (June-August)
- Multi-condition filtering for precise insights

#### **Task 6: App Opens Impact Assessment**
- Engagement rate comparison: Tweets with vs without App Opens
- Business-hour focused analysis (9 AM - 5 PM weekdays)
- Dual time-window visibility for strategic review
- Comprehensive metric correlation study
